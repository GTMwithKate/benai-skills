# Intake (step 2)

Ask in one round with AskUserQuestion. Skip anything already answered. Do not start any work without these answers.

## Questions
0. **Mode.** A motion graphic on its own (explainer, logo animation, product demo, reel, intro: go to `references/standalone.md`), or work on an existing video (the stages below).
1. **Stages.** Which of these: edit (cuts), motion graphics and overlays, captions. Any mix.
2. **Video.** File path, folder of clips or YouTube URL.
3. **Goal and platform.** Full edit, social clip, teaser, highlight reel. Output 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1. Target length.
4. **Script.** Do they have copy to match (copy-first) or should the story come from the video (video-first)? Edit stage only.
5. **Editing mode.** Hard cuts (default) or crossfades (trailers and montages only). Edit stage only.
6. **Motion density.** Light 2 to 4 clips per minute, medium 4 to 7 (default), heavy most lines. Motion stage only.
7. **Include or avoid.** Moments to keep or cut, lines to leave on the face, real numbers or names to show, product screenshots to recreate (ask them to drop screenshots and logos into `motion/inputs/`).
