# Check: readability

The universal slice of clarity only. Whether it sounds like the company is the Voice check, do not grade taste here. Flag and suggest.

Look for:

- Sentences so long or tangled they have to be read twice to parse.
- A wall of text with no breaks where the format clearly needs them.
- Jargon stacked with no plain-language anchor anywhere.
- A buried lede: the actual point arrives three sentences late.

For a finding, quote the hard-to-parse span and give a clearer rewrite.

Do not flag short, plain, deliberate style as a problem. Terse is not a defect.

## Grading

- Aligned: a reader gets it on the first pass.
- Drift: a couple of clunky sentences or a missing break.
- Misaligned: genuinely hard to follow, the reader has to work to understand it.
